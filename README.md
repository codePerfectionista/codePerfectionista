  <h3 align="center">What We're Building at SA Healthcare AI</h3>

<br/>

<div align="center">

We are solving the reliability problem in healthcare AI by introducing strict engineering guardrails into agentic workflows:
* **Deterministic Guardrails:** Creating reproducible outputs where guesswork is eliminated.
* **Explicit Edge-Case Handling:** Architecting multi-agent systems designed around the chaos of real-world clinical care.
* **Compliance-Ready Architecture:** Delivering bias-checked, HIPAA-aware, and fully auditable systems that enterprise compliance teams sign off on.

### 🛠️ My Focus & Philosophy
* **Tech Leadership:** Pushing the boundaries of LangGraph, state-machine agent orchestrations, and rigorous LLM evaluation frameworks.
* **Clinical-First Design:** Ensuring AI adapts to the clinician, not the other way around.
* **Open Source & Community:** Believing that the future of responsible AI relies on transparent, community-vetted engineering standards.
